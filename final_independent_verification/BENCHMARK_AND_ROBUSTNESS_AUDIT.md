## 1. DATASET IMPLEMENTATION AUDIT (`dataset.py`)

Inspection of `dataset.py` confirms that `IndustrialDefectDataset` is a **procedural synthetic benchmark generator**, not an image loader for physical factory photography.

### 1.1 Synthetic Generation Mechanism
- **Rolling Metal Background Texture (`_generate_steel_background`):**
  - Synthesizes base metallic gray intensity: $I_{\text{base}} \sim \mathcal{U}(115, 145)$.
  - Horizontal rolling grain texture via low-frequency sinusoids: $I_{\text{grain}} = A \sin(y \cdot f)$ with $f \sim \mathcal{U}(0.05, 0.2)$ and $A \sim \mathcal{U}(3, 8)$.
  - Linear illumination gradients simulating non-uniform factory illumination across strip width: $\Delta I_x \sim \mathcal{U}(-15, 15)$, $\Delta I_y \sim \mathcal{U}(-10, 10)$.
  - High-frequency micro-roughness noise: $\epsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$ with $\sigma \sim \mathcal{U}(2, 4)$.
- **Defect Morphology Synthesis (`_synthesize_defect`):**
  Systematically models the six defect categories of the benchmark NEU Surface Defect Database:
  1. *Crazing (Cr):* Branching dendritic micro-cracks generated by random walk angles and binary dilation with Gaussian intensity attenuation.
  2. *Inclusion (In):* Localized particulate elliptical inclusions with dark slag cores and diffuse halo margins.
  3. *Patches (Pa):* Low-contrast surface blemishes generated with Perlin-style smooth gaussian blobs ($\Delta I \approx 10\text{--}25$).
  4. *Pitted Surface (PS):* Multi-pinhole clusters generated via Poisson point distributions ($N \in [10, 30]$ pinholes).
  5. *Rolled-in Scale (RS):* Textural oxide scale indentations with high-frequency internal variance.
  6. *Scratches (Sc):* High-aspect-ratio directional gouges with parabolic width variations.
- **Morphological Boundary Generation (`_compute_boundary`):**
  Online computation of target boundary contours using a flat $3 \times 3$ structuring element $\mathbf{K}_3$:
  $$\mathbf{B} = (\mathbf{M} \oplus \mathbf{K}_3) - (\mathbf{M} \ominus \mathbf{K}_3)$$
  This produces a precise 1- to 2-pixel wide contour target for boundary loss supervision ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{bound}}$).

### 1.2 Seed Discipline & Sample Counts
- Training partition: 700 synthetic image patches ($256 \times 256$, grayscale).
- Validation partition: 150 independent synthetic image patches per condition.
- Seed initialization:
  $$\text{RNG Seed} = \begin{cases} \text{seed} & \text{if mode} = \text{'train'} \\ \text{seed} + 1000 & \text{if mode} = \text{'val'} \end{cases}$$
  For default `seed=42`, validation uses seed 1042, preventing sample overlap between training and evaluation partitions.

### 3.1 Scientific Preservation of Negative Findings
The manuscript faithfully retains these negative results:
- **Low-Contrast Disadvantage:** Section VI-A explicitly explains that MSBA-Net's boundary stream relies on localized intensity gradients. Under low-contrast lighting attenuation ($\Delta I \to 0$), the auxiliary head fails to extract sharp edge features, reducing BGFF attention gating effectiveness.
- **Motion Blur Disadvantage:** Optical blur destroys high-frequency edge cues, leaving dense multi-scale architectures like U-Net++ more resilient.
- **Engineering Requirements:** Section VI-B explicitly states that deep networks require plant-level engineering controls (pressurized air-knife enclosures to clear oil mist, pulsed LED strobe illumination, strip tension stabilizers) to maintain imaging conditions within operational bounds.
